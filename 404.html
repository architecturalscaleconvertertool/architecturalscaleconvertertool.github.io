Architectural Scale Converter

Architectural Scale Converter is a free, open-source tool that converts architectural, engineering and metric drawing scales in the browser.

  • No sign-up, no cookies, no tracking. Drawings stay in your browser.

  • Results are for reference. Check critical dimensions against the dimensions written on the drawing.
